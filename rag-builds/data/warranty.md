# Warranty Policy

## 1. Coverage Period

All electronics carry a one-year warranty against manufacturing defects, starting from the date of delivery. The warranty period does not pause or extend due to repairs or replacements during the original term.

Accessories such as chargers, cables, and earbuds are covered for six months. Batteries are covered for one year or five hundred charge cycles, whichever comes first.

## 2. What Is Not Covered

The warranty does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, or damage caused by using a charger or accessory that did not come with the product. It also excludes cosmetic wear, scratches, and dents that do not affect functionality.

Damage resulting from unauthorized repairs, tampering, or opening the device is not covered. Software issues caused by third-party applications or modifications fall outside the warranty scope.

## 3. How to Raise a Claim

To raise a warranty claim, log in to the website or app, go to Support, and select Warranty Claim. You will need the order ID, a clear photo of the issue, and a short description of what happened. A support ticket is created and assigned to a technician within twenty-four hours.

Alternatively, you may call the warranty helpline between ten am and eight pm on working days. Have your order ID ready before calling to speed up the process.

## 4. Inspection and Pickup

Once a claim is accepted, a pickup is arranged within three working days in serviceable areas. In locations without pickup service, customers may drop the device at the nearest authorized service center. A copy of the warranty card or order confirmation must accompany the device.

The inspection typically takes three to five business days. Customers receive an email with the outcome and the next steps.

## 5. Resolution Options

If the defect is confirmed, the first option is repair at no cost. If repair is not possible, a replacement of the same or equivalent model is offered. If neither repair nor replacement is feasible, the order amount is refunded to the original payment method.

Replacement devices carry a fresh warranty of one year from the date of replacement delivery.

## 6. Out-of-Warranty Repairs

Devices outside the warranty period can still be repaired for a fee. The estimated cost is shared before any work begins, and customers can choose to proceed or collect the device unrepaired. Unrepaired devices are returned within seven days of the estimate being declined.

## 7. Warranty Transfer

The warranty is non-transferable. It applies only to the original purchaser and the device registered under their account. Proof of purchase may be requested at any point during a claim.
