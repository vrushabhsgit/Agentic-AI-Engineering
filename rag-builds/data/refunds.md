# Refund Policy

## 1. Refund Window

Customers may request a refund for any product within thirty days of the delivery date. The request must be submitted through the Returns section of the website or mobile app. Requests made after the thirty-day window will not be accepted under any circumstances.

For digital products such as software licenses, e-books, and online course access, the refund window is shorter — seven days from the date of purchase. After seven days, digital products are considered consumed and no refund applies.

## 2. Condition of Returned Items

Physical items must be returned unused, in their original packaging, with all accessories, manuals, and free gifts included. Items that show signs of wear, installation, or activation will be inspected and may be rejected. In that case, the customer will be notified by email within two business days and given the option to collect the item or accept a partial credit.

## 3. Processing Time

Once the returned item passes inspection, the refund is processed in five to seven working days. The amount is credited to the original payment method. UPI and net banking refunds typically appear within three days. Card refunds may take up to ten working days depending on the issuing bank.

Cash on delivery orders are refunded to the customer's registered bank account. A cancelled cheque or passbook copy may be requested to verify account details before the transfer is initiated.

## 4. Non-Refundable Items

The following categories are excluded from the refund policy: perishable goods, opened personal care products, gift cards, downloadable software, items marked as final sale at the time of purchase, and products damaged due to misuse or negligence.

## 5. Cancellation Before Shipping

If an order has not yet shipped, the customer may cancel it directly from the Orders page. The full amount is released immediately — no return shipping is needed. For prepaid orders, the refund appears on the same payment method within twenty-four hours.

## 6. Partial Refunds

In some cases, a partial refund may be offered instead of a full return. This applies when the product is functional but the customer is dissatisfied with a specific feature. The partial amount is decided by the support team based on the nature of the complaint and communicated before any action is taken.

## 7. Escalation

If a refund request is denied or delayed beyond the stated timeline, customers may escalate by writing to support at escalate at company dot com. Include the order ID, the date of the original request, and a brief description of the issue. Escalations are reviewed within three business days.
