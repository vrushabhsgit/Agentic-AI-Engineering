# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## 1. Order Cut-Off Times

Orders placed before two pm on a working day are packed the same day. Orders placed after two pm move to the next working day. On weekends and public holidays, all orders are processed on the next working day regardless of when they were placed.

Bulk orders of ten or more items are subject to an additional processing delay of one to two business days, as they go through a separate warehouse queue.

## 2. Delivery Timelines by City

Metro cities — Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune — receive deliveries in two working days after packing. Tier-two cities take four to five working days. Remote pin codes and hilly regions can take up to eight working days.

Delivery partners do not attempt delivery on Sundays in most service areas. If a delivery falls on a Sunday, it is rescheduled to Monday without any extra charge.

## 3. Free Delivery Threshold

Free delivery applies automatically on all orders above nine hundred ninety-nine rupees. For orders below that amount, a flat shipping fee of forty-nine rupees is added at checkout. The fee is non-refundable even if the order is later cancelled.

Corporate and institutional orders may qualify for negotiated shipping rates. Contact the B2B team for a custom quote before placing the order.

## 4. Tracking

A tracking link is sent by email and SMS as soon as the courier picks up the parcel. Tracking updates appear in real time on the carrier's website. If no movement is recorded for forty-eight hours after pickup, customers should contact support with the tracking number.

Lost shipments are investigated by the logistics team. Customers are kept informed at every step, and a replacement or full refund is arranged once the loss is confirmed — usually within five business days of the report.

## 5. Address Changes

Address changes are possible only before the order is packed. After packing, the delivery address cannot be modified. If the customer is unavailable at the time of delivery, the courier makes two more attempts over the next two days before returning the parcel to the warehouse.

## 6. International Shipping

International shipping is currently available to select countries only. Delivery times range from seven to twenty-one business days depending on the destination. Customs duties and import taxes, if any, are the responsibility of the customer and are collected by the local carrier at delivery.

## 7. Shipping Restrictions

Certain items — batteries, liquids, and pressurized containers — cannot be shipped by air and may take longer through surface transport. Customers placing such orders will see the extended timeline at checkout before payment.
